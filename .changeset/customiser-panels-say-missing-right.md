The panels on a product and on an order line say plainly when the reader's role is short of a right, instead of a blank drawing, "Designs: none yet" or "No design on this line": which of the Customiser's tables the role may not read, that the customer's words, logo and photo are hidden from it, and which Customiser role shows it all or makes the proof and the files. The order-line panel makes no proof or file from a design the role cannot read whole, and says so when making the files again is refused for want of the right to remove the earlier ones. The README lists what an app's role needs for each panel.
