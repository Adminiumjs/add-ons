Forms: after sending, a form can go to one of your own landing pages ("Go to a landing page"), picked from the live pages of a landing pages add-on installed beside it. The form keeps the page by its key, so a new address or host never breaks it; Publish says so when the page is not live. Offered with a live landing page; forms stored before read as they always did. Forms now needs Adminium 0.3.65 or newer: an older one refuses a form that ends on a landing page.
