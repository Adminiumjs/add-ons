Customiser: the set-up page has **Shop settings**, where the shop keeps its one-colour line (where light becomes dark when a shopper makes their logo one colour), a new page's bleed and safe line, the thinnest line and the sharpness a picture needs; Adminium's own add-on settings screen draws only settings a manifest declares, and the Customiser keeps its own in a table. An app's role may now change these settings (`appGrants`), and the README's set-up grants keep a shop's owner to its own app's rows: with Maker Shop and Print Shop on one install, neither owner sees, changes or removes the other's set-up, nor makes a row for it, and both still read and fill the starter fonts. The proof-maker's grants read the settings, so its proof uses the shop's own line.
