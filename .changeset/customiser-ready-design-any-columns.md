Customiser: a shop updated from an earlier Customiser could refuse every ready-made design with "This design can't be made as it is", because updating the add-on does not add new columns to what the shop's visitors are already served. The shop's editor now takes a ready-made design whatever columns the shop serves, and the order is still checked in full when it is placed.
