Google reviews, a new add-on: a business's own Google reviews, loaded in the visitor's browser in a sandboxed page, never stored. Offers sells packs online (`voucher-sale-online@1`), sends a gift card on a date, and lets a signed-in customer read and spend what they hold without a code in the browser. Forms has a fifth hide reason, "The author asked", and the fourth now reads "Names someone who isn't on our team". Holiday Calendars attaches to Bookings.
