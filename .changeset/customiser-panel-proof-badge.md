Customiser: the order-line panel says what the Customiser made, not what the shop sent: its badge reads "No proof made" instead of "No proof yet", and a design with no version yet reads "The Customiser has made no proof of this design yet." instead of "No proof sent yet.". A shop may send its own file as a proof from its own app, which the panel does not read.
