On a survey with "Link answers to names" off, the Invitations screen now says that a person answered and no longer shows a date. Adminium keeps no answering time for these invitations, so that an answer cannot be matched to the person it came from. A record's Survey tab now says Answered or Not yet for every survey, with no date; a named survey's exact date stays on its Invitations screen.
