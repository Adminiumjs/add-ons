The Customiser says how far a shop app's own role may go on each of its tables (`appGrants`), so an app's maker or pre-press role makes the proof and the production files without a Customiser role: it reads the shop's set-up, reads a customer's design with its words, logo and photo, adds a proof version and sets its proof file (never the words an order's posting copied), and makes, attaches and replaces a version's files. The settings, the postings and the log of mailed links are given to no app's role. Needs Adminium 0.3.47. The README lists the entries an app's role takes, the set-up page's address, and the set-up page's view a role needs to make files in a starter font.
