Customiser: a shopper can order a ready-made design again. Since 1.0.24 the shop's editor said "This design can't be made as it is" for every ready-made design and refused Add to basket, because the shop's visitors were not served which shop a ready-made design belongs to. They are now, as they already are for a base and a font. Updating the add-on is all a shop needs to do.
