A new add-on, Lead finder: find companies by what they do and where, fill in a company's details from its website and the public registers, and keep the people who work there when you switch that on. Six sources: the website reader (Adminium reads the site), Companies House with your key, the registers of France and Norway with no key, OpenCorporates with your paid key (fill-in only), and Hunter with your key (people only). Each source reaches only its own hosts and gets only its own key. The marks France and OpenCorporates put against sales use are honoured, and a sole trader counts as a person. It keeps the people found, each field's source, and the mail that tells a found person where their details came from, all written by Adminium alone. Two roles: Company finder and Lead finder settings manager. Needs Adminium 0.3.66; installed online only, and not listed yet.
