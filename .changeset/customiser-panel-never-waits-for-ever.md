Customiser: the order-line panel waits for a picture's lightness only so long, and never on a printed page; a one-colour logo whose lightness was not read is said to be drawn in full colour, and a missing or unreadable settings row is said to leave the shop's one-colour line unknown. A blank proof is refused before any version is saved, a design taken into the full editor included. On "Choose a design", a card whose 3D model never comes keeps its flat drawing and no longer holds up the cards after it.
