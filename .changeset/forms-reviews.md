Forms keeps customers' reviews. A reviews table of its own (stars, title, words, the name shown, waiting, published or hidden with a reason, and the shop's reply) and the totals of each thing reviewed, counted from the published reviews by Adminium. An app takes a review through its own `review` word; its roles read and moderate their app's reviews only; its pages read the published reviews (ten a page, newest first, by the thing) and the totals through the app's own key. Needs Adminium 0.3.68.
