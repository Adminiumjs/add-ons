Customiser: two corrections to the 1.0.24 notes. After making a proof or the files, the order-line panel reads again the design itself as well as its versions and their files, not only the versions and files. And in the set-up's Fonts step, a font whose file failed to load is tried again the next time the Fonts step is opened, or when the font's file changes, not on its own while the step stays open.
