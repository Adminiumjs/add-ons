Customiser: the order-line panel reads the shop's set-up (bases, ready-made designs, fonts, colours, features, settings) once, and after making a proof or the files reads again only the design's versions and their files, not all fourteen of its tables. Each "Make the proof" and "Make the files" reads the fonts and the shop's one-colour line as they are then, so a font file or a line changed in another tab since the panel opened is used.
