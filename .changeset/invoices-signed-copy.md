Invoices & Receipts draws a new kind of document, the signed copy: what a customer signed on site when the work was handed over. It names the work, where and when it was done, the checks the customer was shown ("Yes" or "No"), what was still to put right at the moment of signing, the signature itself, who signed and when, and every page ends with a line saying what the copy is and the page's number. The signature is a picture the app's file column holds, handed over by Adminium 0.3.35 or later; the PDF now draws PNG pictures (transparency kept) and JPEG pictures, with no new dependency. As with every document here, the PDF draws Latin letters; an Arabic or Chinese copy is the web page, for printing.
