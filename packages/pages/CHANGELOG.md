## 1.0.31

### Patch Changes

- 9a97ad2: A new add-on, Landing pages: pages for offers and events, each at its own address, with one of your forms on it if you like. Adminium draws every live page for visitors, writes its title and sharing preview, serves its pictures and counts its views; no code of the add-on runs for a visitor. Its one Library page holds the pages as cards or a list with their status and this month's views, the Funnels tab (up to eight steps of pages and forms, each step's views over a range of days, the share that went on, and the rule linked by hand as what happens after), and the page editor. Two roles: Landing pages editor and Landing pages reader. Needs Adminium 0.3.65 on a public https address, and comes with Adminium from the release after it.
