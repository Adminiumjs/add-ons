# Landing pages

Pages for offers and events, each at its own address, with one of your forms on
it if you like. Funnels put a page, its form and its thanks page in order and
show how many views each step gets.

Adminium draws every live page for your visitors, writes its title and sharing
preview, serves its pictures and counts its views: no code of this add-on runs
for a visitor, and nothing is fetched from anywhere else. It needs Adminium
0.3.65 or newer, on a public https address.

It comes with Adminium from the release after 0.3.65 on, as Forms & surveys
and Live chat do, so a server without a disk of its own keeps serving its live
pages after a redeploy; on 0.3.65 itself it is added from Add-ons.

## What it keeps

- **Pages**: each page's name, its address (`/x/pages/<address>`), its one
  language, the copy staff are working on, and the copy visitors see, frozen
  when it is published. A page is a Draft, Live or Off; Adminium refuses to
  publish a copy that does not hold together, that Milo drafted and nobody has
  checked, or whose address is taken.
- **Funnels**: a name, up to eight steps (pages and forms, in order) and, if you
  pick one, the rule that follows a sent form.

Counts are Adminium's own: views, never people, kept as the privacy settings
say.

## Its screens

One page in the Library, **Landing pages**: the pages as cards or a list, with
their status and this month's views; the **Funnels** tab, a funnel's steps with
the views of each, and what happens after; and the page editor.

## Roles

- **Landing pages editor**: makes, changes, publishes and deletes pages and
  funnels.
- **Landing pages reader**: reads pages and funnels, and changes nothing.

It attaches to every app and works with none. A form on a page comes from a
forms add-on installed beside it.
