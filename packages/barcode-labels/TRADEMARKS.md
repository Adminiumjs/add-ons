# Trademarks

**See [`../../TRADEMARKS.md`](../../TRADEMARKS.md).** Every mark any add-on in
this repository references is listed there, once, with the affiliation
disclaimer and the no-logo rule that bind all of them — and this add-on's own
detail lives there as a section rather than in a sixth copy of the same page.

This add-on references **no mark at all**, which is exactly why it still has a
section over there: an absent line is indistinguishable from a forgotten one.

The section is worth reading for this package in particular. A barcode is where
a reader most expects a supplier to turn up, and there are three separate
directions one could have come from — the symbologies, the numbers themselves,
and the label stationery. None of the three is named, and each of the three is
checked rather than asserted.
